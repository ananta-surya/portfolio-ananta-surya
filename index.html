Undergraduate Thesis

PUDO Facility Management Analysis on Lempuyangan Station

A traffic engineering study analyzing the performance of Lempuyangan Road, Yogyakarta, affected by Pick-Up and Drop-Off (PUDO) activities using PTV Vissim microscopic simulation across multiple management scenarios and a 20-year traffic projection.

Key Highlights

  • Microscopic Traffic Simulation: Developed a PTV Vissim model validated with GEH < 0.2 and MAPE 7.3%, replicating real-world mixed-traffic conditions on an urban arterial road
  • Performance Analysis: Peak hour degree of saturation reached DJ 0.910, exceeding PKJI 2023 threshold of 0.85, with vehicle speed dropping to 9.8 km/h due to high side friction from PUDO activities
  • Scenario Evaluation: Tested four PUDO management scenarios across a 20-year traffic projection; prohibiting on-street parking reduced average queue length by 62.2% and stabilized travel time under 2 minutes

Thesis Supervisor: Ir. Mukhammad Rizka Fahmi Amrozi, S.T., M.Sc., Ph.D., IPM.
